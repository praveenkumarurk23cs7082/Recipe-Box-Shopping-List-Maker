On Your Birthday, 2025

My dearest love,

There are so many words I want to say to you today, and yet none of them feel quite enough. You are someone who came into my life and quietly rearranged everything — in the best possible way.

This birthday is different for us. We can't be together the way I wish we could. I can't hand you a gift or sit beside you or pull you into a hug. But I want you to know that this distance doesn't diminish what I feel for you — not even a little. If anything, it has made me realize just how much you mean to me.

You are passionate, brilliant, funny, and so incredibly kind. Watching you love the things you love — the races, the football, the little details of life — makes me love you more every day. You care so deeply, and it shows in everything you do.

On this birthday, I hope you feel celebrated. I hope you feel loved from wherever I am. I hope this little corner of the internet reminds you that someone, somewhere, thinks you are absolutely extraordinary.

Here's to you. Here's to us. Here's to every birthday we'll celebrate together from now on — in the same city, in the same room, finally.

With all my love, always and forever,

Your Girl ❤️
